Install as a Chrome extension

Replace your New Tab page with the Quote Board — every new tab becomes a calm, fullscreen space for your own quotes.

Quote Board — Chrome Extension Unpacked extension package (.zip)
Download .zip
Why not just the Chrome Web Store? This extension isn't published there yet, so for now it installs the developer way — a few extra clicks, but completely safe, and it's the same process any Chrome extension starts with before it's published.
1
Click on three points button to open the menu.
2
Menu is opened. Choose "Extentions" option.
3
Click on "Manage Extentions"
4
"Extentiones" opens. Then turn on "Developer mode" by clicking toggle button in the corner.
5
Now as new options appear, press on "Load unpacked" button.
6
!!! Please unzip/unpack "quote-board-extension" folder first. After new window appears, search for unziped/unpacked "quote-board-extension" folder where is stated "extention" folder. Then choose it and and click blue "Choose folder" button.
7
The extension card appears in your list. Make sure its toggle is blue/on, then open a new tab to see your board and click allow to let the board stay on.
